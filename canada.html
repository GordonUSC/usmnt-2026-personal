SQUAD RECONCILIATION

01

The confirmed late changes

Tajon Buchanan and Cyle Larin withdrew injured. Theo Corbeanu and Marcus Caldeira joined; this is Caldeira’s first senior call-up. Jeevan Badwal returned to Vancouver with a hamstring strain.

02

The updated camp page

Jamie Knight-Lebel, Justin Smith and Luca Petrasso appear on the updated camp page. Knight-Lebel is a Bristol City player on loan at Motherwell. Alfie Jones and Derek Cornelius are absent from that updated list; no diagnosis is asserted here.

03

Unavailable for the window

The original federation announcement listed Alphonso Davies and Maxime Crépeau as unavailable. The camp page still includes Buchanan and Larin as participants, so it cannot certify tonight’s final squad or bench.
