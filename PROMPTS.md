# USA 26 · The Prompt Series
### Taking every page of the personal USMNT site, and the whole experience, to the level this summer deserved

Written by Darby, August 1, 2026. Built from three research passes: (1) the verified record of the full 2026 tournament, (2) the 50 most impactful uses of Claude and AI around this World Cup, and (3) a primary-source sweep of Gordon's own photos, messages, tickets, and calendar from June 10 to July 31.

The one-line thesis, borrowed from the best thing the AI World Cup wave figured out: **not "the 2026 World Cup," but "our 2026 World Cup."** Same tournament as everyone else. Made smaller and warmer by who was around the table.

Four design pillars from the research:
1. **The archive is the product.** The best Claude World Cup projects (SeatSidekick, the wc26 MCP server, the published build transcripts) treated real data as the star. Our real data is photos, texts, and tickets.
2. **Graded honesty.** The projects people loved showed what they believed before, and what happened after, and did not flinch. The Belgium loss gets its true weight. The hope gets its true weight too.
3. **Every memory addressable.** One link should carry one exact moment to one exact person.
4. **Authenticity is the scarce good.** In the tournament of the deepfake flood, everything on this site is real and labeled. Real photos with dates. Real quotes with names (or anonymized). Nothing invented, ever.
