How to play · why the world changes

Focus the scene to use arrows or W A S D. Left rewinds, right advances; up and down change the heart and the atmosphere. Walk into a ball to shoot. On a phone, hold the direction buttons or drag the scene. Tap an era to explore directly. No memory is locked behind a score.

Space cheers, M calls MATCHDAY, H sends hearts. The toy buttons reveal each era’s keys. Escape pauses. Your discoveries and place are saved locally; “Walk again” keeps discoveries. Sound is opt-in.

Playable experiment: time changes the renderer, and mood changes the world. The graphics grow from a Pong-inspired paddle era to polygon rendering. Anime is a stylistic branch, not a hardware generation. The journey ends in a real photograph. This keeps the act of remembering at the center of the game. Both modes use the same era character renderer. The HD and anime environments use the existing commissioned AI artwork, now wired into the challenge run too; the finale uses Gordon’s existing photograph without cropping away his face.
