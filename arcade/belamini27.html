    The controls · the decisions · the rules
    Move / sprint
    Arrows or WASD. Hold Shift for a burst; the teal bar under your player is stamina. Release to recover. Sprinting carries the ball farther from your feet and makes a rushed shot less accurate.
    Pass / switch
    Z or Space passes toward the way you face. The dotted white lane shows the likely receiver. Without the ball, Z selects another defender.
    Call / release a runner
    Tap E on the ball to send a teammate beyond you. The teal dotted path shows the run. C plays into that space; it can be intercepted. Passes count when a teammate receives them.
    Set / shoot
    Hold X, aim left or right, then release in the teal power zone. Let the amber first-touch ring turn white before shooting. Close, settled shots earn a “set shot”; power alone does not guarantee a goal.
    Press / cover
    Hold E without possession to send a teammate to the ball. Move your own defender into the passing lane. Pressing consumes that teammate’s stamina. X is a slide tackle.
    Shape / coach
    Q cycles balanced, wide and compact spacing. Wide stretches a block but leaves larger gaps; compact offers shorter passes. V toggles tactical guides. P pauses; Escape returns to the circuit. Click the pitch before using game keys.
    Stars / replay
    Three objectives per scenario. Replays keep your best star total and score for each. Everyone can access all five challenges. The same seeded opposition decisions make each start comparable; your inputs change how the match develops.
    Points
    100 per objective, 20 per completed pass, 60 more for a completed through ball, 40 per settled shot, 250 per goal you score, and 40 per successful press or tackle. Each scenario keeps its best score and best star total separately.
    Arcade rules
    Eleven players, simplified physics, rolling restarts. No offside, fouls, substitutions or real roster ratings. The 2027 scenarios, teams and results are fictional. They are not fixtures, predictions or calibrated football analysis.

    Why this experiment: football expertise should help your play. The sequel rewards finding a free player, shaping a passing lane and choosing the moment to shoot. Built from the original BelaMini engine and code-drawn art; sound is optional. The historical 26 game keeps its own story and records.
