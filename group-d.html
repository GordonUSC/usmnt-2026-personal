Three matches · Twelve days · Two venues · Group won

Group D.

We won it. Six points: 4-1 over Paraguay on June 12, 2-0 over Australia on June 19, and a 3-2 loss to Türkiye on June 25 with a heavily rotated side, after the knockouts were already clinched. USA finished top of Group D. This page holds the results, and the scouting we wrote going in, graded honestly. For the last two matches, make your call before you read the result. I had to.

Match 1 · Jun 12 · SoFi · Final

Paraguay. Done.

Fri Jun 12 2026 · SoFi Stadium, Los Angeles · Group D · Match 1
USA v Paraguay
USA 4 · Paraguay 1
The opener. The plan worked, and then some.
Result: USA 4, Paraguay 1
Group D · Match 1 · Won

The plan worked, and then some. Paraguay own goal off Damián Bobadilla at 7'. Folarin Balogun's brace at 31' and 45'+5', the first American to score twice in a World Cup game since 1930. Giovanni Reyna at 90'+8'. Paraguay pulled one back at 73'. Christian Pulisic was the creator, forcing the own goal and assisting Balogun's first before leaving at halftime with calf tightness.

After one match, USA topped Group D with 3 points and a +3 goal difference. Australia came next on June 19 at Lumen Field, Türkiye on June 25 back at SoFi. The scouting below is what we read going in.

Paraguay (La Albirroja)
Coach Gustavo Alfaro · 4-2-3-1 / 4-4-2

Star: Miguel Almirón (Atlanta United pace, the counter-attack threat).

Key spine: Gómez (CB, captain), Alderete (CB), Cubas (DM), Enciso (creator), Sanabria (ST).

The shape

Defensive organization first. One of the stingiest defenses in CONMEBOL qualifying: 10 conceded in 18 matches. They scored just 14, the lowest among auto-qualifiers. They are built to frustrate, not to win 3-2.

How they hurt USA

  • Almirón on the counter when USA's fullbacks push too high
  • Set pieces in the second 45 when USA's back line is stretched
  • Patient, structured pressure that forces low-percentage passes

How USA wins

Patience. Don't force the early goal. Win the midfield. Trust Pulisic and Balogun to find one half-chance and convert. If 0-0 at 60, this becomes about who breaks character first.

Grade: We wrote "built to frustrate, not to win 3-2" and then it was 3-0 to us at halftime, so the stingiest defense in CONMEBOL qualifying broke character in the 7th minute. Trusting Pulisic and Balogun to convert was the one call we nailed, and I will take being wrong about the patience part every single time.

Match 2 · Jun 19 · Lumen Field · Final

Australia. Done.

Fri Jun 19 2026 · Lumen Field, Seattle · Group D · Match 2 · Noon kickoff
USA v Australia
USA 2 · Australia 0
Watched from LA at noon, texting "watching soccer" at 12:19.

Before you scroll: noon in Seattle, no Pulisic. What do we do?

A noon kickoff in Seattle and a clean sheet. Burgess, one of the back three we scouted below, turned one into his own net at 11', and Alex Freeman rose for a header at 43'. Pulisic missed the match entirely, his calf kicked in training, and Pochettino answered with a 3-5-2 we never saw coming: Freese; Richards, Ream, Robinson; Dest, McKennie, Adams, Tillman, Freeman; Pepi, Balogun. 62 percent possession. Knockout rounds clinched with a game to spare.

I watched this one from LA at noon, texting "watching soccer" at 12:19 like that was a normal Friday and not the day we punched our ticket. The scouting below is what we read going in.

Australia (Socceroos)
Coach Tony Popovic · 3-4-2-1

Star: Nestory Irankunda (pace, finishing, breakout player).

Key spine: Ryan (GK), Souttar / Burgess / Circati (back 3), Bos (LWB), McGree (creator).

The shape

Settled back three. Wing-backs are the engine. They press the full 90 minutes and they don't fade. Beat Denmark and Tunisia at Qatar 2022. Reached the R16. Tony Popovic took over in the middle of a crisis and went unbeaten through qualification.

How they hurt USA

  • Pressing intensity forcing USA into errors in the build
  • Irankunda's pace against any unsettled centerback
  • Set-piece moments through their back-three height

How USA wins

Match the press intensity. Use Adams and McKennie to bypass the first wave. Make the wing-backs defend by pinning them with Robinson and Weah. Australia is dangerous as long as they're moving. Make them defend their box.

Grade: "Make them defend their box" held up beautifully: 62 percent possession, and the first goal came off one of their own defenders. What we never saw coming was Pochettino answering their back three with one of ours, a 3-5-2 without Pulisic, which is a humbling reminder that the coach reads more film than I do.

Match 3 · Jun 25 · SoFi · Final

Türkiye. The one I saw.

Thu Jun 25 2026 · SoFi Stadium, Los Angeles · Group D · Match 3
USA v Türkiye
Türkiye 3 · USA 2
My night. Inside SoFi, next to Pete, on the ticket he transferred me on June 7.

Before you scroll: group already won, everyone rested. The Türkiye finale goes...

With the knockouts already clinched, Pochettino rotated heavily: Turner in goal, Reyna and Berhalter in the eleven. We struck first, Trusty off a corner at 3', and Güler answered at 10'. Barış Alper Yılmaz put Türkiye ahead with a knee finish off Kökçü's shot at 31'. Berhalter pulled us level at 49', and Kaan Ayhan got the final touch on the winner at 90'+8'. USA still won Group D with 6 points.

This was my night. I was inside SoFi, next to Pete, on the ticket he transferred me on June 7, the one that had me texting friends "Not me crying in an uber on the way to a soccer game." Quinn was in the building too, in a different section, because of course he was. The scouting below is what we read going in.

Türkiye (Ay-Yıldızlılar)
Coach Vincenzo Montella · 4-2-3-1

Star: Arda Güler (Real Madrid, 21, the truth).

Key spine: Çakır (GK), Demiral / Bardakçı (CB), Çalhanoğlu (deep playmaker), Güler (no. 10), Yıldız (LW).

The shape

Creative spark via Güler between the lines. Çalhanoğlu sets tempo from deep and runs the best dead-ball routines on the continent. Yıldız stretches the back line on the left. They came through European playoffs and look sharp.

How they hurt USA

  • Çalhanoğlu set pieces (corners, indirect free kicks)
  • Güler between the lines if USA's midfield drops too deep
  • Yıldız one-vs-one against any non-Robinson fullback

How USA wins

Press Çalhanoğlu. Don't let him pick his head up. Adams or McKennie has to be in his lap for 90 minutes. If Çalhanoğlu plays free, Türkiye scores twice.

What this game means

If both sides come in tied on points, this is the actual group-decider. Winning Group D matters for the R32 path. Easier 3rd-place opponent if we win, harder if we don't.

Grade: We wrote "if Çalhanoğlu plays free, Türkiye scores twice" and they scored three, so call that an undercount, and yes, Güler is the truth: 10th minute, I saw it with my own eyes. The group-decider fear never came true, because we walked in with the knockouts already clinched, which is the better way to be wrong.
