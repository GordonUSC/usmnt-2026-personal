Since July · in order

Seventy-eight days, receipted.

Mon · Jul 6 · Lumen Field

Belgium 4, USA 1. The coach said it plainly.

"I think we were not good enough, it wasn't our day." Pochettino, afterward, with his contract running out. I read that quote in Stoughton, Wisconsin, and for about a month nobody knew if he was staying.

Sun · Jul 19 · MetLife

Spain 1, Argentina 0.

Ferran Torres at 106'. The biggest World Cup ever played ended twenty miles from my table in New York.

Mon · Aug 3

He stayed. Through 2030.

U.S. Soccer signed Pochettino to a new contract through the 2030 World Cup, with a bigger job than before: advising the youth national teams, the U-17 and U-20 World Cups, and the road to the 2028 Olympics. A philanthropic gift from Citadel founder Ken Griffin helped fund it. His words: "there is tremendous potential to make the Men's National Team program even stronger." A full four-year cycle, from the first friendly to the next World Cup.

Tue · Aug 4

Gio Reyna goes to France.

The man who toe-flicked the 90'+8 goal against Paraguay left Borussia Mönchengladbach for Strasbourg on a five-year deal. A fresh start, which is the kindest phrase in soccer.

Summer · the transfer window

Middlesbrough became America's club.

Sebastian Berhalter, who played all five of our World Cup matches, left Vancouver for Middlesbrough. Max Arfsten followed him from Columbus. Zavier Gozo went from Real Salt Lake to Crystal Palace for a reported $15 million. Diego Kochen went on loan from Barcelona to Lyngby in Denmark. Damion Downs went on loan from Southampton to St. Louis City.

Deadline day

Balogun, main character again.

Our ten-men hero had a move from Monaco to Everton agreed, went to the medical, and then walked away from it after the deadline. He is still at Monaco. The man cannot do anything quietly, and I have made my peace with that.

Thu · Sep 17

The first roster of the new cycle: 28 names, 13 never capped.

Thirteen World Cup players and thirteen uncapped players, a dozen of them called up for the first time, an average age of 22 years and 9 months. Cavan Sullivan, 16, from the Philadelphia Union. Mathis Albert, 17, of Borussia Dortmund, from Greenville, South Carolina, already the youngest American ever to play in the Bundesliga. No Pulisic, no McKennie, no Balogun this time. Pochettino: "This is the beginning of a fresh start for everyone."

Sun · Sep 20

Pepi out, Downs in.

Ricardo Pepi was withdrawn injured after PSV's 3-2 loss to Twente. Damion Downs took his place. That leaves twelve World Cup players in camp.

June and July · 2030

The next one: Spain, Portugal and Morocco.

I will be 59. I have already started the math on which friend is getting me into which room. Mike, you have a head start.
