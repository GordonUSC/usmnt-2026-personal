01 / BUILD THROUGH PRESSURE

Can the next player face forward?

Watch the first receiver, not only the goalkeeper. A clean first pass helps only if the next player can turn, combine, or choose a sensible longer option.

Look for: a supporting angle and a second pass ready before the first arrives.

Why this matters for this U.S. team

Official data: against Belgium, FIFA recorded 126 U.S. line breaks to Belgium's 79, but expected goals of 0.33 to 1.99. Getting the ball forward did not create comparable chances. Expected goals is a model-based estimate of chance quality, not a score that “should” have happened.

Our reading: follow what happens after the press is beaten, how chances are created, and who protects the space when the ball is lost. Those questions tell you more than possession alone.
